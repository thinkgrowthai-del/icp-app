Pain
The problem in the customer's own words. Not yours. Not marketing copy. Theirs.
⚡
The 5pm Thursday testWould a frustrated ops manager actually say this at the end of a long week? If it sounds like a landing page — rewrite it.
✗ Avoid this

"Inefficient approval workflows slow down business velocity and impact organisational performance."

✓ Aim for this

"We approved a $15k vendor contract over Slack DMs and nobody followed up for three weeks. The invoice showed up and nobody knew who signed off."

Write the sentence they'd say to a colleague — specific, plain, frustrated

Quick Reference — Key Concepts
ICP
Company + person most likely to buy, get value, and stay. A filter, not a wish list.
Firmographics
Headcount, ARR, stage, industry, geography. Always use specific numbers.
Technographics
Tools they already use. Each signals readiness and workflow maturity.
Buying Trigger
An event that creates urgency: new hire, funding, headcount jump.
Pain
The problem in the customer's own words. If it sounds like copy — rewrite it.
Persona
Specific title + seniority + responsibility. Must be searchable in Clay.
Disqualifier
A rule that removes near-misses. As valuable as a qualifier.
5pm Thursday test
Read pain aloud. Landing page? Rewrite as frustrated plain English.
ICP read-back
A new SDR acts on this without follow-up questions. If they can't — fix it.
